# Subscription Plans

## Overview

ExDocs offers three subscription tiers designed for Indian exporters at different stages of growth. Plans are billed monthly or yearly (with a discount).

**Access:** Top nav → "Subscription"

---

## Current Plan Indicator

Shows the user's:

- Active plan name + status
- Usage & Limits: PDF quota each billing cycle, Items & Buyers per account usage
- Trial status (e.g., "You're on a 7-day trial")

---

## Billing Cycle Toggle

**Monthly** / **Yearly** toggle. Yearly saves ~16%.

---

## Plans

### Basic — ₹2,099/month

For small exporters getting started.

**Included modules:**

- Exports
- Reports (limited)
- Domestic
- Inventory
- Find Buyers

**Add-ons available:**

- Production (+₹1,499/mo)
- Import (+₹1,499/mo)
- Postshippers – Early Access (contact team)

---

### Advanced — ₹4,899/month _(Best Value)_

For growing export businesses.

**Includes everything in Basic plus:**

- Full Reports
- Domestic
- Production
- Inventory
- Find Buyers

**Add-ons available:**

- Import (+₹1,499/mo)
- Postshippers – Early Access (contact team)

---

### Professional — ₹9,099/month

For large-scale export operations.

**Includes everything in Advanced plus:**

- All Modules: Exports, Reports, Domestic, Inventory, Production, Import
- Find Buyers (unlimited)

**Add-ons available:**

- Postshippers – Early Access (contact team)

## Promo Code

Each plan has an optional promo code field at checkout:
**"AGENT OR PROMO CODE (OPTIONAL)"** field + Apply button

---

## Subscription Actions

- **Subscribe to Basic / Advanced / Professional** — Direct CTA buttons
- Payment is automatic on billing cycle
- Can cancel anytime
- Prices exclude GST; final amount including GST deducted at checkout

---

## Trial

- 7-day free trial available for new accounts
- Trial PDFs are watermarked with "ExDocs Trial"
- All features accessible during trial
- No credit card required (varies)

---

## Find Buyers Feature

Accessible from all plans via the **"Find Buyers"** button in the top navigation.

An AI-powered buyer discovery tool to find international buyers for your products. Not fully documented in current screenshots but appears as a prominent feature across all plans.
